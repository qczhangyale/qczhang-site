## Abstract

Public production derivative of the exact-certificate and reproducibility archive for the three Complementarity-First Electromagnetism manuscripts. It contains contact-safe paper fixtures, finite exact certificates, independent correction suites, claim-to-certificate maps, environment records, and a unified verifier. It does not claim historical priority, external human peer review, independent external replication, or a derivation of full QED from primitive complementarity.

## Where it sits in the release

CEM-D1 is the evidence layer for the electromagnetism arc — exact certificates, reproducibility files, claim-to-evidence maps and verification tools for [CEM-P1](/papers/cf-14-complementarity-before-electromagnetism/), [CEM-P2](/papers/cf-15-conditional-maxwell-reconstruction/) and [CEM-P3](/papers/cf-16-radiation-helicity-quantization/).

It is the only Release II record published as a **Dataset** rather than a preprint.
